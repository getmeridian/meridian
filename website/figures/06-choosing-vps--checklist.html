Before you deploy
  • Public IPv4; meridian scan is IPv4-only
  • TCP and UDP 443 open in the provider's cloud firewall too
  • Fresh Debian or Ubuntu LTS, SSH key, root or passwordless sudo
  • 2 GB free disk, clock within 30 seconds
  • Hourly billing: a blocked address costs an hour
  • No exit inside the censored country
Test before you commit
  • meridian check IP: target, 443, reachability, OS, disk, clock
  • Deploy, then meridian test and test --probe
  • From inside users' networks: home line and mobile
  • Test again after a few days; blocks can arrive late
  • Keep a second exit at a different provider and network
